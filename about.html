About Tesdash

An independent, privacy-centric driver companion designed for electric vehicles. Fast, local, and engineered for true performance driving.

🏎️ Why We Built Tesdash

Modern electric vehicles have incredible central screens, but essential real-time driving information—speed, regenerative braking, blind-spot closures, and trip metrics—often demands glancing away from the road ahead.

Tesdash was built by passionate electric vehicle drivers to deliver a customizable, ultra-responsive digital instrument cluster right in the driver's natural line of sight. Inspired by classic Porsche 911 3-dial cockpits and BMW M aerodynamic styling, Tesdash provides the tactile intimacy of motorsport gauges combined with next-generation electric vehicle telemetry.

⚡️ Core Highlights & Features

  • 10 Hz Real-Time Telemetry Direct Bluetooth Low Energy communication with 100 ms update cycles. No server roundtrips or cellular lag.
  • 100% On-Device Privacy Zero cloud servers. Vehicle key negotiation happens via hardware ECDH secp256r1 + AES-GCM on your device.
  • Apex 3-Dial Cockpit Porsche-inspired analog-digital dial arrangement with hero speedometer, companion battery, and dynamic navigation.
  • Multi-Card Modular Dashboards Six bespoke luxury card designs: Contemporary OEM, Prism Diamond, Aero Blade, Curved Horizon, and OLED Ghost.
  • Post-Drive Summary & Savings Comprehensive trip receipts displaying distance, duration, Wh/km efficiency, and calculated fuel savings.
  • Thermal Defense & Smart Scaling Windshield mount protection that throttles rendering dynamically under summer sun to avoid iPhone overheating.
  • Dynamic Island & Live Activities Lock-screen and Dynamic Island widgets keep your live speed and battery level visible while running other apps.
  • Driver Fatigue Monitor Intelligent driving duration tracking with gentle, non-intrusive break reminders every 2 hours on long highway trips.

🛡️ Our Privacy Guarantee

We believe automotive software should never monetize driver telemetry. Tesdash does not track your location, does not record your routes, and does not require user accounts. Your vehicle stays strictly between you and your car.
