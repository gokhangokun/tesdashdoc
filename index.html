Support & Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pair Tesdash with my vehicle?

Make sure Bluetooth is enabled on your iPhone. Launch Tesdash, enter your vehicle's 17-character VIN, and tap Connect. Bring your physical Tesla key card to the center console card reader when prompted to authorize the secure pairing key.

Can I try Tesdash without being in my car?

Yes! Simply tap "Start Demo" on the welcome screen. Tesdash will launch in fully interactive Demo Mode with simulated live speed, battery usage, navigation, and G-force metrics.

Does Tesdash drain my phone battery?

Tesdash is optimized for automotive efficiency with native SwiftUI rendering and proactive thermal management. However, because the screen remains on while driving, we recommend keeping your device connected to a wireless or USB charger.
