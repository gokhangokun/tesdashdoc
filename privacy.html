Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Core Privacy Commitment: Tesdash does NOT collect, sell, or transmit your personal data, location, or vehicle telemetry to any remote server or cloud database. All processing happens 100% locally on your iOS device.

1. Information We Process

Tesdash is designed with a strict zero-telemetry, privacy-first architecture:

  • Vehicle Telemetry: When paired with your vehicle via Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE), Tesdash reads speed, battery level, estimated range, tire pressures, power usage (kW), gear state, climate status, and door closure states. This data is processed strictly in memory on your device to update your dashboard gauges and is never transmitted off your device.
  • Location Data (GPS): Tesdash uses your device's GPS location solely to compute instantaneous vehicle speed, heading, trip distance, and to render local 3D chase camera navigation using Apple MapKit. Location data is never logged to external servers or used for user tracking.
  • Vehicle Identification Number (VIN): Your VIN is stored locally in your device's encrypted Keychain / Secure Storage to facilitate automatic BLE reconnection to your vehicle. It is never transmitted to us or third parties.

2. Bluetooth Low Energy & Cryptography

Direct vehicle communications utilize end-to-end local cryptography conforming to Tesla's official Vehicle Command protocol (ECDH secp256r1 key agreement and AES-GCM authenticated encryption). Pairing keys and session tokens remain exclusively inside your device's Secure Enclave / Keychain.

3. Third-Party Services & Integrations

  • Apple In-App Purchases (StoreKit 2): When you purchase subscriptions or lifetime licenses, transactions are handled directly by Apple Inc. We do not receive or store your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing address.
  • Apple Music & iTunes Search API: If enabled, Tesdash displays media metadata (track title, artist) from your device's music player. Album art queries are anonymously fetched from public Apple iTunes lookup endpoints without sending any personal identifying information.
  • MapKit: Map tiles, reverse geocoding, and routing services are provided directly by Apple MapKit under Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because Tesdash does not operate remote user accounts or databases, we store no personal data on external servers. You can permanently delete all locally stored vehicle pairing keys, nicknames, and dashboard preferences at any time by navigating to Settings > Reset Dashboard Configuration or by deleting the app from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Tesdash does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. The application is strictly an automotive auxiliary tool intended for licensed motor vehicle operators.
