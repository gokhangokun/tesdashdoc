Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Core Privacy Commitment: Tesdash is completely ad-free and telemetry-free. Tesdash does NOT integrate third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, does NOT operate remote user accounts, and never uploads your vehicle telemetry or driving history to cloud servers. Vehicle communication occurs 100% locally via Bluetooth Low Energy between your iOS device and your vehicle.

1. Information We Process Locally

Tesdash is architected on a privacy-first, on-device model:

  • Vehicle Telemetry: When connected via direct Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE), Tesdash reads telemetry (speed, power consumption in kW, battery percentage, estimated range, tire pressures, cabin/ambient temperature, gear, door/closure states). This information is evaluated in real time on your device and optionally cached locally for drive summaries and Home Screen widgets—never transmitted to any external server.
  • Location & Motion Data (GPS & Sensors): While active in the foreground and when Location permission is granted, Tesdash accesses your device's location, elevation, and motion sensors to calculate vehicle speed, compass heading, road grade, session distance, and 3D chase camera navigation via Apple MapKit. Your device's CoreLocation coordinate (at the precision you authorize in iOS Location settings—Precise or Approximate) is also passed to Apple WeatherKit when fetching optional local weather and wind conditions below.
  • Local Media Artwork Matching (On-Device): Tesdash never sends song titles, artist names, or playback metadata to external servers or catalog search services. If you have granted Media & Apple Music access on your iPhone, Tesdash inspects the local Music app's currently playing item strictly on-device and displays matching album artwork only when both track title and artist match the vehicle's active playback. Without permission or a verified local match, Tesdash displays a built-in source icon.
  • Local Diagnostics (MetricKit): Performance and crash diagnostics provided by Apple's on-device MetricKit framework are logged locally via Apple's unified logging system for troubleshooting on your device and are never uploaded to external crash servers.
  • Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) & Pairing Keys: Your VIN and cryptographic pairing credentials are kept exclusively inside your iOS device's encrypted Keychain / Secure Storage.

2. External Network Requests & Platform Services

Tesdash operates offline-first and connects only to Apple platform services for the optional features below—without user accounts or tracking identifiers:

  • Current Weather & Wind (Apple WeatherKit): When optional Pro weather or Aero & Wind widgets are used in real driving mode and Location permission is granted, Tesdash requests current weather conditions (temperature, humidity, weather condition, wind speed, and wind direction) from Apple WeatherKit using the device's CoreLocation coordinates. Requests are shared across weather and wind widgets, throttled (at most once every 15 minutes or 5 km of travel), and are not performed in Demo Mode or in the background. No personal identifiers or VINs are included. See Apple Weather Legal Attribution for weather data source notices.
  • Apple In-App Purchases (StoreKit 2): Optional Pro subscriptions, Lifetime unlocks, and voluntary developer tips are processed exclusively by Apple via StoreKit 2. Tesdash does not collect, process, or store payment card details.

3. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland:

  • Zero Tracking & Zero Ad Profiling: Tesdash does not collect personal data for advertising, does not track you across third-party apps or websites, and does not share personal data with data brokers.
  • Your Rights & Control: Because Tesdash stores vehicle pairing credentials, drive logs, and settings solely on your device, you maintain full control at all times. You may revoke Bluetooth, Location, Motion, or Media & Apple Music permissions in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security or erase local data directly within Tesdash.
  • Supervisory Authority: You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority if you have concerns about how your data is handled.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because Tesdash maintains no cloud database or user accounts, we retain zero user data remotely. You can delete cached driving history, remove your paired vehicle and Keychain credentials in the Garage view, wipe app history from Settings, or remove all local data by uninstalling Tesdash from your device.

5. Vehicle Communication & Cryptography

All direct communications with your vehicle adhere to Tesla's official Vehicle Command cryptography protocol, including Elliptic-Curve Diffie-Hellman (ECDH) P-256 key agreement and AES-GCM-128 authenticated encryption. Cryptographic keys are protected by the iOS Keychain with device-bound access control.

6. Children's Privacy

Tesdash is intended solely for licensed motor vehicle operators and does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions).
